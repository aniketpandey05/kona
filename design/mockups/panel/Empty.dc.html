A page you are only passing through

Nothing kept here, and no space set

Most pages are like this one. You open them, read a little, and leave without keeping anything, and that is the normal case rather than the exception.

So this is what Kona looks like almost all of the time it is installed: a single small mark in the corner, and nothing else. No panel, no empty list, no prompt asking whether you would like to highlight something.

An empty panel on every page in the world is the fastest way to be uninstalled. The product has to be willing to be invisible.

What to look at

This is 2d, and it is the state Kona is in most of the time. No count, because there is nothing to count. No orange strip, because no space is set. One mark, slightly faded.
