How an amoeba moves

Revised 11 March · this article has been rewritten

An amoeba moves by extending a pseudopod, and then flowing the rest of itself into the space the projection has made.

The technique was first described in the 1830s, using instruments that were extraordinary for their time, though the article no longer says much about who built them.

Feeding uses the same motion, enclosing a particle in a vacuole that is then digested inside the cell.
