What to look at

The nav already lists the spaces, so this grid earns its place by carrying what a list cannot: the note, the counts, the last-touched, the open question. The active space wears the orange ring and says so in words — 2e’s rule, that what is going where is never implied — and it keeps the same orange dot in the nav, so you can see it from anywhere. One space has no note at all, because 1d settled that nothing is required — and 20 cut the rest away: a space is a place to put highlights together, with no write-up and no findings. Nothing here is a thread, a poll or a ping either; those arrive only when a space is shared, which is Team.
