What to look at

Also in your library is 12’s hardest rule made visible. Editing the case never repaints what you already marked, so colours in use can outnumber the pens — and that list is where a colour goes when you retire it, rather than nowhere, which is how somebody would otherwise lose track of 54 highlights.

The name writes itself from the hue. Teal, not Disagree — pens have colours, not labels, and the meaning stays yours.

Two previews, light and dark, because a mark is painted over a page whose background Kona does not control. That is also what the 60% ceiling is for — the one piece of 12 still open. Here it is as a slider that simply ends, with a reason rather than an error.
