What rent control actually did

The San Francisco expansion of 1994 is the cleanest natural experiment anybody has, because the cutoff was a building’s age rather than a tenant’s circumstances.

Rent control reduced displacement for sitting tenants and reduced the supply of rental housing by fifteen per cent, which is the finding both sides quote and neither finishes quoting.

Landlords converted units to condominiums or redeveloped them, and the effect on new construction was smaller.
