Privacy Policy — Kona

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Kona does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third-party service of any kind. The extension and the phone app make no network requests.

What is stored, and where

Everything Kona saves stays on the device that saved it, in your browser's own storage:

  • The text you highlight, and a copy of it kept so it survives the page changing.
  • Notes and tags you write, and the colour you chose.
  • The address and title of the page or chat the highlight came from.
  • Your theme preference, and which sites you have switched Kona on or off for.

None of this is readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes it.

Site access

Kona runs on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini out of the box, because that is what it is for. Any other site is switched on by you, one at a time, and can be switched off or forgotten again. Kona reads the text of a page only to find your highlights and put them back where you left them.

Data you export

The backup and Markdown files are written by you, to wherever you choose. They are the only copies of your highlights that ever leave the browser, and Kona plays no part in where they go afterwards.

Children

Kona is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the history is public in the project repository.

Contact

Questions or reports: open an issue on GitHub.
